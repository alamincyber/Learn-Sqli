## 🌐 About the Project

**Learn Cyber Security** is an independent educational initiative designed to help Bengali-speaking beginners understand cybersecurity through structured learning paths, easy explanations, and practical documentation.

Our goal is to make cybersecurity education easier to understand, accessible to everyone, and focused on real-world knowledge.

Whether you are completely new to cybersecurity or looking to improve your technical skills, this platform aims to support your learning journey.
